Small text-to-text game I will code in C++ just for fun

## The situation
You're operating a small exploration spacecraft.

Your onboard computer has detected an unidentified distress signal coming from somewhere in a nearby sector.

Your mission is to locate the source, approach it, and recover its transmission.

But there's a problem.

Your spacecraft has limited energy. Every action matters.

If you run out of energy before recovering the signal, the mission fails.
